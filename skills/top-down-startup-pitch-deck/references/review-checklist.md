# Pitch deck review checklist

Use on every deck before delivery, and as the scorecard in Review mode (Pass / Fix + a few words for each of the six areas).

## 1. Answer first
- [ ] Cover and Tagline together say what the company does, for whom, and the ask (investor) or offer (product).
- [ ] The tagline is a statement of value, not a slogan with no content.

## 2. Storyline
- [ ] Every headline is a full-sentence takeaway, not a label.
- [ ] Read alone and in order, the headlines tell the whole pitch.
- [ ] Sequence follows the framework in `deck-structure.md`, or deviations have a reason.
- [ ] One idea per slide; the content proves the headline and nothing else.

## 3. Evidence
- [ ] Research was planned from the solution: the sources chosen are the authoritative ones for this industry, problem and geography.
- [ ] Every external figure's source passes the trust test (origin, method, independence, currency, fit).
- [ ] Every external figure was opened and matches in number, unit, geography, year and definition, and is traced to its origin.
- [ ] Headline and investment-critical figures (market size, core pain statistic, growth) are corroborated by a second independent trusted source.
- [ ] No vendor blogs, statistics roundups or self-serving vendor studies cited.
- [ ] Figures older than 3 years are dated on the slide or replaced.
- [ ] Forecasts are labelled as forecasts with the publisher.
- [ ] Every internal figure is labelled company data.
- [ ] SAM/SOM and any savings or ROI numbers show their arithmetic in the notes.
- [ ] No invented testimonials, quotes, customers, logos, awards or partnerships.

## 4. Investor essentials (investor deck)
- [ ] Why this team, with specific credentials.
- [ ] Market sizing separates TAM, SAM and SOM; SOM is bottom-up.
- [ ] Why now is backed by dated external forces.
- [ ] Unit economics: price, gross margin, and CAC/LTV or payback, with assumptions.
- [ ] The ask: amount, use of funds, milestones reached.

For product decks instead: pricing is clear, the call to action is one specific next step, competitor facts are cited and dated.

## 5. Design
- [ ] One typeface; no italics.
- [ ] Sizes within the ranges in `design-system.md`; nothing oversized.
- [ ] One accent colour, used sparingly.
- [ ] Nothing clipped, overflowing or overlapping; numbers on one line.
- [ ] Source footer on every slide with a sourced figure; Sources slide at the end.

## 6. Delivery
- [ ] Speaker notes on every slide with talking points and full citations.
- [ ] Sources ledger delivered.
- [ ] Open items listed: `[needs source]`, figures to confirm, claims softened or removed.

## Verdict rules
- **Investor-ready** (or **Prospect-ready**): all six areas pass.
- **Needs fixes**: areas 1–2 pass; issues are in evidence details, design or delivery.
- **Needs rebuild**: area 1 or 2 fails, or any fabricated or unverifiable figure sits on a headline.
