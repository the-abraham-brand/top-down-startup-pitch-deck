# Design system: minimal tech

Quiet, dark, spacious. The numbers and headlines carry the slide; decoration does not. If the user supplies brand colours, fonts or a logo, use those in place of the defaults below and keep every other rule.

## Palette (default)

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Charcoal | 181714 |
| Cards / panels | Lifted charcoal | 201E1B |
| Accent (labels, key figures, rules) | Warm gold | C9A844 |
| Primary text (headlines) | Off-white | F6F5EC |
| Secondary text | Warm grey | 8A8374 |
| Subtle borders | Dark line | 2E2B26 |

Use the accent sparingly: section labels, the key figure on each slide, thin rules, and one highlighted card at most. Never more than one accent colour.

With a brand palette: background = darkest brand colour (or white for light decks), accent = the strongest brand colour, text = high-contrast neutral. Check contrast: body text at least 4.5:1 against its background.

## Typography

- One typeface for the whole deck. Use the brand font if it is available where the deck will be opened; otherwise Georgia (safe everywhere). A clean sans (e.g. Manrope, Inter) is fine when it can be embedded or is installed.
- **No italics anywhere.** Use weight and colour for emphasis.
- Restrained sizes (16:9, 10 × 5.625 in):

| Element | Size (pt) | Weight / colour |
|---|---|---|
| Section label ("03  THE MARKET") | 8–9, letter-spaced | Regular, accent |
| Headline | 26–32 | Bold, primary text |
| Cover headline | 32–36 | Bold, primary text |
| Key figure | 24–30 | Bold, accent |
| Claim beside a figure | 12–14 | Bold, primary text |
| Body / consequence line | 10–12 | Regular, secondary text |
| Source footer | 7–8 | Regular, secondary text |
| Page number | 8 | Regular, secondary text |

## Layout

- Margins 0.5 in on all sides. Content never touches the edges.
- Every content slide: section label top-left, a short accent rule under it, headline below, content, source footer at the bottom, page number bottom-right.
- Layout patterns that suit the framework:
  - **Stat stack**: three rows of big figure + claim + consequence, separated by hairline rules (Pain Points, Market).
  - **Stat row**: three or five figures side by side with a thin accent rule above each (Market, Solution proof points).
  - **Numbered proofs**: 01 / 02 / 03 in accent, title, one line of support (Team, Why Now).
  - **Tier cards**: three cards, one highlighted with an accent border (Unit Economics, Pricing).
  - **Comparison table**: your column in accent, the rest neutral (Competition).
- Logo on the cover and the closing slide only (plus an optional small watermark).
- Generous negative space; if a slide feels full, move content to speaker notes.

## Production checks

- Render every slide to an image and look at it before delivering.
- No text clipped, wrapped mid-number, overflowing its box, or overlapping another element.
- Numbers never break across lines ("₹14,999" on one line; widen the box or reduce size one step).
- Source footers are present on every slide with a sourced figure.
